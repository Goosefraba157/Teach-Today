Hich-Quatity WRS IMPLEMENTATION
Implementation recommendations for WRS are summarized in the table below.
Wilson Reading System® High-Quality Implementation
Student Focus Students in grades 2-12 and adults who have significant reading and writing deficits at the
word-level, and/or poor automaticity and fluency, especially those with a language-based
learning disability, such as dyslexia
Group Size 1:1 or small homogeneous groups (ideally up to 4 students). Group size up to 6 appropriate
in some settings (ie, with a highly skilled WRS teacher)
WRS Lesson 10 Parts divided into 3 Blocks of instruction:
+ Block 1 (Parts 1-5: Word Study/Foundational Reading Skills)
+ Block 2 (Parts 6-8: Spelling/Foundational Writing Skills)
+ Block 3 (Parts 9-10: Fluency and Comprehension)
One full lesson consists of all 3 Blocks of instruction (20-30 minutes per Block to complete).
Must follow scope and sequence with progression based upon mastery.
Length and Minimum number of lessons is 2 complete lessons per week (see above).
Frequency 1:1 instruction:
+ 45 minutes, 4-5 times per week 2
+ 60-90 minutes, 2-5 times per week
Small-group instruction:
+ 45-60 minutes, 4-5 times per week
H + 75-90 minutes, 2-5 times per week
+ Optimal intensity: 90 minutes/5 times a week
: Lesson length and/or frequency should be at the higher ranges for students who are more
than 2-3 grades behind in reading as well as for students with complex cognitive profiles
(see Getting Underway, Student Identification and Pretesting, later in this manual). In some
cases, WRS replaces a student's classroom reading instruction. If so, the WRS class should be
scheduled 90-120 minutes, 5 times per week.
Duration Depending upon student profile and intensity (group size, lesson length and frequency),
it may take 2-3 years (or more) to complete all 12 Steps.
Setting Wilson lessons should occur in a space conducive to optimal learning. (See the section on
Key Principles of Instruction.)
