Stupent Mastery AND PROGRESSION FROM STEP TO STEP
To determine progression from one Step to the next, students take an end-of-step assessment that is both formative
and summative. These end-of-step assessments determine a student's mastery and understanding of the concepts
taught in the specific Step and identify any specific areas that need further instruction. Students are assessed in
phonetic word reading accuracy and automaticity, marking current concepts, high frequency word reading,
independent silent reading, passage oral reading fluency and comprehension, and dictation. Students must reach a
specific mastery level. If they do not, additional instruction in areas of difficulty is necessary before going on to the
next Step.
To determine readiness to progress from one Step to the next, students complete a Step assessment during two
consecutive lessons. Specific instructions are provided in the Step Instruction section of this manual for the following:
End-of-Step Assessment Lesson 1
+ Step concepts - marking
+ Phonetic word reading accuracy and automaticity
+ High frequency word reading
+ Independent silent reading
End-of-Step Assessment Lesson 2
+ Dictation
+ Passage fluency and comprehension
