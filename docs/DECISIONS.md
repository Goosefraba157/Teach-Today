## Recovery backup authority

- **Decision:** Daily and weekly recovery backups always originate from an immediate complete snapshot of the current local app state. Neither Firebase nor a Firebase-normalized/shared payload may be read, selected, or substituted while creating a local or Google Drive recovery backup.
- **Destinations:** The same immutable JSON text is written to the local iPad Files Daily/Weekly locations and, when authorized, to the Google Drive Daily/Weekly locations. Native Files verifies its received bytes against SHA-256; Google Drive must be re-downloaded and SHA-256-verified before the app reports that destination successful.
- **Verification:** Recovery artifacts carry only a local-source/mode label and non-identifying evidence counts. The visible backup status must report those counts. A destination failure is an explicit backup warning, never a reason to read, overwrite, merge, or substitute a different data source.
- **Boundary:** Firebase may remain an explicitly separate legacy/browser synchronization and recovery service while it is being retired or redesigned, but it is never a recovery-backup authority. Supervisor Sheets are derived reporting copies, never a recovery source.
- **Attendance evidence:** Attendance day views are projections by the evidence record's own saved day. Every charting, dictation, and Sections 6–8 record saved that day is visible regardless of its plan/lesson association; a plan link supplies only contextual labeling. Month `held` counts remain explicit attendance confirmations; instructional evidence never infers or writes attendance.
- **Stage identity:** The native shell's document marker and trusted native bridge establish Stage identity for both application and storage layers; route query parameters are supplementary navigation state. Losing a route marker must not cause a storage-authority downgrade or a migration attempt.

## Lesson identity and continuity

- **Decision:** A group may have one explicitly active lesson at a time. The app must identify it by lesson number, group, session day, and a teacher-selected date.
- **45+45 sessions:** Day 1 and Day 2 belong to the same lesson number but store separate dates. Day 2 defaults to the next Monday-Thursday instructional date and remains editable. The teacher may complete the lesson as-is at any point; unfinished sections are valid.
- **Switching groups:** Leaving a group preserves its lesson, active day, completed/skipped sections, saved versions, and scroll position. Returning offers a clear choice to continue, complete, or preserve it as incomplete and plan a new lesson.
- **Teaching action:** `Start Planned Lesson` saves and opens the exact Customize preview in Present mode. `Continue Lesson` resumes the existing saved lesson in Present mode. Building or reviewing a draft does not by itself mean that teaching has started, and a new unsaved lesson may not replace or coexist with another unfinished official lesson for the same group.
- **Teacher completion authority:** The teacher may complete a lesson as taught even when some sections are skipped or no student evidence was recorded. The Home continuity action must therefore mark the selected lesson Complete before preparing a new planner; it must not label or store the teacher's completed lesson as Incomplete. The Wrap-Up completion remains the usual end-of-lesson path because it also saves the final summary and completed PDF.
- **Home continuity actions:** Resume and close-as-incomplete must resolve the selected group and lesson again by stable ID at the moment of the tap. The native iPad flow uses an in-page close confirmation rather than depending on a JavaScript system-confirm callback. Closing is idempotent: if background synchronization already made the rendered lesson terminal, the action clears only its stale active pointer and exposes the planner without rewriting the terminal status. An actual close changes only that lesson and its active session to `Incomplete` and preserves its evidence and history.
- **Continuity lookup:** Opening from a selected group resolves that group's plan before any global fallback, because legacy or combined records may reuse an ID. A combined participant normally follows its host link; if that host copy is absent on the local-only classroom device, its complete participant lesson snapshot remains teachable and must open without rewriting evidence or inventing a replacement lesson.
- **Home return lifecycle:** Returning from Profile or another app page through browser history must not trust the iPad webview's cached Home DOM. On a persisted page restoration, Home reloads the current local application state, resets transient confirmations/errors, and renders the selected group again. This is a view refresh only and does not rewrite lesson or student records.
- **Corrections:** Current-year official lesson dates and numbers may be corrected from the selected group's lesson-history control only after explicit confirmation. Corrections do not alter student evidence or instructional content.
- **Next lesson defaults:** Completing a lesson as-is or preserving it as incomplete and planning another prepares the next automatic lesson number on the next Monday-Thursday instructional date. Teachers may override the date, including Friday when needed.
- **Test separation:** Demo teaching should use a group explicitly marked as a demo group so test saves do not enter official group continuity. Routine UI must not expose a broad school-year lesson reset.

Missing records remain blank; automatic district explanations apply only to past/current weekdays within the verified instructional season, with explicit attendance taking precedence. - **EdPlan CSV reporting:** Attendance Central exports the selected year's confirmed attendance into date rows and student columns. Present cells use only the session's saved plan IDs for substeps, with curriculum titles/targets as concise descriptions; planned dates, charting evidence, and current group substeps never infer instruction. Explicit absences are labeled, missing/no-session values remain blank, missing lesson links and contradictory group records are flagged. Exports are reporting copies for review/entry, with no assertion of EdPlan import compatibility. Manual Drive exports are saved under Teach Today Backups/Reports and re-downloaded/hash-verified; they never alter the reference Sheet or attendance ledger.

- **Decision:** Attendance is explicit, not inferred. Opening, planning, presenting, navigating, or saving a lesson never marks a student present. A student calendar may show a new record only after the teacher confirms a held session; an explicitly recorded no-session day remains blank.
- **Attendance-only recovery:** A backup may add confirmed attendance only through an explicit-date, preview-first path. The current iPad state always wins: recovery may add a session only when that group/date has no existing session, and it must not modify attendance already present or any charting, lesson, dictation, notes, roster, or nonselected-date records. On Stage, a verified native checkpoint is required both before and after the additive database write.
- **Daily identity:** One group has at most one attendance session per local calendar date. That session may reference one or more lesson numbers and any combination of Day 1 and Day 2 portions; lesson portions are instructional phases, not calendar-day boundaries.
- **Statuses:** Every rostered student must have an explicit present/absent value when the teacher confirms. Confirmed attendance is stored by both the current teacher-facing name and permanent private student ID when available.
- **Fast default:** Opening an unconfirmed attendance date presets every rostered student as present in the unsaved editor so the ordinary workflow is to toggle only absences. Nothing becomes an attendance record until the teacher confirms.
- **Coverage inference:** Lesson coverage is advisory and may be detected from meaningful section interactions such as buttons, inputs, charting, drawing, or section navigation. Scrolling alone is never evidence of instruction. Detected coverage does not create attendance and remains manually adjustable because preparation and teaching interactions can look alike.
- **History view:** The correction surface shows four calendar weeks with explicit confirmed, no-session, and legacy-needs-review states. Blank dates remain blank but may be selected to add a deliberate correction.
- **Corrections and preservation:** Prior dates remain editable. Corrections retain a private audit snapshot of the earlier value. Legacy attendance records are preserved unchanged and remain readable until the teacher deliberately replaces a specific date with an explicit confirmed or no-session record.

## Student identity and school years

- **Decision:** Use permanent private student IDs to connect records across school years while keeping each year's groups and activity separate.
- **Reason:** Students may leave or remain, while longitudinal growth must remain available without merging yearly operational data.
