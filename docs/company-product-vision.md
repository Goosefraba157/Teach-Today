# Teach Today App Company/Product Vision

## Product Identity

Teach Today App is becoming a professional educational learning game platform for dyslexia intervention, structured literacy, and teacher-led reading instruction.

The product should feel engaging and game-like to students while functioning as a serious instructional system for teachers.

## Core Promise

Teach Today turns structured reading and spelling intervention into motivating digital practice without weakening the instructional purpose.

Fun is part of the learning design. It should increase attention, effort, repetition, and confidence. Fun must never replace evidence, teacher judgment, or mastery.

## Product Principles

1. Teacher-controlled, student-friendly.
2. Structured literacy underneath, game-like experience on top.
3. Evidence and mastery matter more than points.
4. Games must reinforce real decoding, encoding, fluency, vocabulary, or comprehension goals.
5. Student privacy matters. Use avatars, nicknames, and class-safe identity patterns.
6. Teachers need simple tools that work during real intervention time.
7. Students should feel like they are entering an exciting learning world, not filling out worksheets.
8. Every activity should save useful instructional data.
9. Visual design should be polished, consistent, reusable, and intentionally sourced.
10. V2 should be built like a product platform, not a pile of patches.

## What We Build

- Teacher lesson planning and assignment tools
- Student game-like practice tied to lessons
- Decoding and encoding activities
- Word building, word marking, dictation, fluency, vocabulary, and comprehension tools
- Evidence tracking and mastery gates
- Teacher review dashboards
- Student motivation systems that support practice and persistence

## What We Do Not Build

- Random reading games with no lesson connection
- Points systems that imply mastery without evidence
- Activities that hide student errors from teachers
- Art or themes that look polished but do not support instruction
- Public copies of copyrighted instructional text or protected source material

## V2 Product Direction

V2 should use the current app as a learning prototype, but the new foundation should be centered on:

- Lesson plans
- Student assignments
- Activity attempts
- Teacher review
- Evidence summaries
- Mastery decisions

The student side can feel playful. The teacher side must remain clear, fast, and instructionally precise.
