# Accessibility And Privacy Notes

## Product Commitment

Teach Today is for students who may find reading and spelling unusually difficult. Accessibility and privacy are core product requirements, not later polish.

## Accessibility Principles

- Keep instructions short and clear.
- Use large, readable text for student activities.
- Avoid cluttered screens during active practice.
- Provide strong visual focus on the current task.
- Support keyboard, touch, and pointer interaction where possible.
- Avoid relying on color alone to communicate correctness or status.
- Use audio carefully, with visible alternatives where practical.
- Allow reduced motion for animations.
- Keep teacher dashboards scannable and not overloaded.

## Dyslexia-Friendly Design Notes

- Use consistent layouts.
- Keep line lengths comfortable.
- Avoid unnecessary decorative text.
- Give students enough spacing around letters, tiles, words, and answer areas.
- Make correction states calm and constructive.
- Avoid timers unless the instructional purpose requires fluency or automaticity.

## Privacy Principles

- Protect student identity in student-facing and shared views.
- Prefer nicknames and avatars over full names and photos.
- Store only data that supports instruction, progress, safety, or required reporting.
- Make teacher review status clear before student data affects mastery.
- Avoid exposing sensitive progress details in public or shared displays.

## Data To Treat Carefully

- Student names
- Student IDs
- Lesson history
- Attempt records
- Error patterns
- Audio recordings
- Teacher notes
- Fluency and comprehension notes
- Mastery decisions

## V2 Data Rule

Every saved student activity record should have a clear instructional purpose and a clear privacy reason for being stored.

## Copyright And Source Material

Private instructional manuals can guide app structure, lesson flow, and fidelity decisions. The app should not expose protected manual text or reproduce copyrighted instructional materials publicly.
